Pilla

A bilingual Persian and English touch-typing tutor with a 150-lesson curriculum and a butterfly avatar.

How it works

Pilla is a React and TypeScript app packaged for Windows with Electron. A lesson builder generates 150 lessons per language, a typing engine measures WPM and accuracy, and XP evolves a butterfly avatar. Everything runs locally, with no account or cloud.
