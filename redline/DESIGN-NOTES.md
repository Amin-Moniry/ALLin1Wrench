# REDLINE / Design notes

## Identity
This is a proposal for Amin Moniry's ALLin1Wrench portfolio, not a generic multi-tool dashboard. Preserve the existing crimson #DC143C, dark red #8B0000, and near-black identity. Replace the intro-only landing with direct access to work, skills, technical journey and contact. Typography, negative space, restrained asymmetry, and a tactile crimson ribbon carry the new art direction.

## Design research
https://www.awwwards.com/sites/gil-huybrecht — documented portfolio emphasis on typography, gallery and microinteractions. Used as a principle reference, not as a copied layout or a claim that REDLINE has won an award. Other discovery results were not treated as inspected visual references.

## Visuals
The ribbon artwork is generated for this proposal and hue-adjusted to the existing crimson identity. Device mockups are CSS concept treatments, not screenshots of actual project interfaces. They are labelled accordingly. No logos, testimonials or client credentials have been invented.

## Release boundaries
This proposal must be reviewed before merging. Do not auto-publish or auto-merge. CNAME, legacy html/css/js/pics, sitemap, robots and original LICENSE are preserved. Email integration is deliberately unconfigured. Coming Soon launch dates are illustrative. The website's NoDerivatives license remains unchanged; the owner requested this proposal.
