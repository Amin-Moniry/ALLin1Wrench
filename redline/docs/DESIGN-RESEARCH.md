# REDLINE 03 — design research

## Scope, honestly stated
- **100 distinct Awwwards archive submission screenshots visually reviewed**, using the first 100 unique entries from five Site of the Day archive pages.
- **103 distinct detail/preview pages successfully loaded and parsed**, from 146 attempted URLs. These are a partly overlapping set, not 103 additional live-site visual audits. Failed loads are excluded.
- Four live websites opened: Dennis Snellenberg, Lusion, Bruno Simon and Locomotive. Initial visual inspection reached the Dennis and Locomotive hero; Lusion and Bruno showed loading states at the initial capture. Do not describe those captures as complete experience audits.
- Awards below are copied from the visible archive badge text. Honors are not silently promoted to SOTD. The portfolio does not claim an Awwwards award.
- The submitted archive images are compositions chosen by their authors. They support composition/color/typography observations, **not** claims about scroll behavior, speed or mobile accessibility. No third-party design imagery is embedded in the portfolio.

## Synthesis → implementation
1. Oversized typography / editorial portfolio references → clear personal manifesto, not an opaque entry screen.
2. Gallery and archive references → five deep case-study pages plus a compact secondary repository index.
3. Object/3D-led identities → original crimson portfolio ribbon retained; avoid downloading a 3D engine just for a decorative hero.
4. Cinematic/full-image narratives → real source screenshots, each explicitly labeled with provenance.
5. Diagrammatic/technical designs → five selectable architecture flows and capabilities linked to evidence.
6. Experimental personal sites → two lightweight local playground interactions; no simulated production AI.
7. Clean utility interfaces → keyboard-search, native dialogs, native FAQ disclosure, reduced motion, readable Persian RTL.
